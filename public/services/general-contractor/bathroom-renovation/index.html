Expert Bathroom Renovation in Manhattan, NY

Bathroom renovation in Manhattan comes with layers most other cities don't have. Co-op and condo buildings follow strict rules around plumbing stack locations, wet-over-dry restrictions, and board-required alteration agreements. At Trimoco LLC, we work within those constraints from day one. We review your building's alteration agreement before any planning begins. We coordinate with managing agents, handle DOB filing requirements for plumbing and structural work, and keep your project board-compliant at every step. Our scope covers full gut renovations, fixture replacements, waterproofing, tile work, and layout changes where the building allows. Manhattan's pre-war building stock adds another layer: aging plumbing risers, plaster walls, and tight elevator access all affect how a job gets done. We plan around those realities so you avoid costly surprises mid-project. If you own a co-op or condo in Manhattan and want a bathroom that works better and looks the part, call Trimoco LLC to get started.

Common questions about bathroom renovation in Manhattan, NY.

Layout changes are possible in Manhattan, but they depend on your building's plumbing stack location and wet-over-dry restrictions. Trimoco LLC reviews these constraints before any plans are drawn so you know what is achievable in your specific building.

The wet-over-dry rule is a building board policy that prevents placing a bathroom or kitchen above a dry space like a bedroom or living room in the unit below. Most Manhattan co-op and condo buildings enforce this rule, and it directly shapes what layout changes are possible in your apartment.

A DOB permit is required in Manhattan when bathroom work involves plumbing changes, structural modifications, or electrical rerouting beyond simple fixture replacement. Trimoco LLC handles the filing coordination so your project meets New York City requirements.

We apply waterproofing systems that meet the standards many Manhattan co-op and condo boards require as part of their alteration agreement approval. Proper waterproofing protects your neighbor's unit below and prevents moisture damage behind finished walls.
